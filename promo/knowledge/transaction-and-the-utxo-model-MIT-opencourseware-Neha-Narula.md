* **[00:07:37]** So it's kind of like what we were doing before, when we saw the banks, and we were talking about banks keeping track of how much funds people have. * **[00:07:54]** And so in the account-based model, users have certain amounts or balances associated with their usernames. And when a transaction comes in, like Alice wants to give $5.00 to Bob, then what might happen is that the nodes in the network which are all maintaining, in a unified way, this balance sheet, will end up saying, OK, Alice had 10 and Bob had zero. * **[00:08:21]** And so what the nodes would do here to see if this transaction was valid would be they would check the signature to make sure that Alice actually authorized this transaction, and they would also make sure that Alice had the funds to spend.
